# Example Lender Ltd — Arrears and Collections Policy

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE. Written for agent-lab smoke tests and golden-set examples.
> Not real regulatory guidance and not any real lender's policy.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all unsecured personal loans issued by Example Lender Ltd to customers resident in the United Kingdom. It covers the period from the first missed contractual payment until an account is either brought back up to date, placed on a repayment arrangement, or closed.

## 2. Arrears stages

| Stage | Days past due | Primary action |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Early | 1–29 | Friendly reminder by SMS and email within 3 working days |
| Mid | 30–59 | Phone contact attempt and an income and expenditure review offer |
| Late | 60–89 | Formal arrears letter and referral to the specialist support team |
| Default | 90+ | Default notice issued |

An account moves to the next stage automatically at the start of the day it crosses the threshold.

## 3. Default notice

A default notice is issued when an account reaches 90 days past due and no repayment arrangement is in place. The notice gives the customer at least 14 calendar days to remedy the breach before any further action. Default notices are always sent by post and copied to the customer's online account.

## 4. Repayment arrangements

Customers may agree a repayment arrangement at any stage. An arrangement:

- is based on a completed income and expenditure assessment;
- lasts for a maximum of 12 months before it must be reviewed;
- freezes interest and fees for as long as the customer keeps to it.

If two consecutive arrangement payments are missed, the arrangement is cancelled and the account returns to the stage it would have reached without the arrangement.

## 5. Customers in vulnerable circumstances

Any agent who identifies signs of vulnerability (for example bereavement, serious illness, or a recent job loss) must flag the account. Flagged accounts are paused from automated contact for 30 days and handled only by the specialist support team.

## 6. Contact limits

No more than 3 outbound contact attempts per week are made on any account, across all channels combined. Contact is never made before 8:00 or after 20:00 UK time, and never on Sundays or bank holidays.

## 7. Fees

A single late-payment fee of £12 may be charged once per arrears stage. No fees are charged while a repayment arrangement is being kept or while an account is flagged as vulnerable.

## 8. Escalation and complaints

Customers can escalate any collections matter to a team leader. Complaints are acknowledged within 5 working days and receive a final response within 8 weeks, after which the customer may refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.
